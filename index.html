§01Profile

I run the layer under the applications. Ten years of Oracle and MySQL databases, Linux and Unix servers, VMware virtualization, enterprise storage, and the middleware and ETL pipelines that sit on top.

Day to day that means sizing server and storage infrastructure against what the application and database actually need, then carrying it through installation, migration, upgrade, and tuning until it is stable enough to hand to the operations team.

I have led teams of system administrators and database specialists, written the technical proposals behind projects we won, and spent plenty of time on the unglamorous half: preventive and corrective maintenance, incident troubleshooting, RCA, firmware and patching, and the documentation that lets the next person pick a system up without calling me first.
